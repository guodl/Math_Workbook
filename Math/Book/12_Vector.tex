	\tcbline
			 The vector equation of line AB: $ \utilde{r}  =  \begin{pNiceMatrix}1 \\ 2 \\ 3\\ \end{pNiceMatrix} +  \lambda  \begin{pNiceMatrix} 5 \\ 3 \\ 7\\ \end{pNiceMatrix} $\\
 			 $mx+c$ is not a complete equation of a line. It is only an algebraic expression. To represent a straight line, the dependent variable  y must be included, giving the correct equation: \\
                         $    y=mx+c $\\  
             When writing a vector equation, the position vector $\utilde{r}$ should appear on the left-hand side. Since  $\utilde{r}$ denotes the position vector of any point on the line, it cannot be omitted. For example:\\
                         $ \utilde{r} =\utilde{a}+λ\utilde{b} $  \\   
			 just as $y$ is required in the line equation $y=mx+c$, the vector $\utilde{r}$ must be shown explicitly on the left-hand side of a vector equation.\\
    \textbf{Common Mistakes:}\\[0.6cm]

		 \end{sArrow}$		   
    \end{warningEnv}
